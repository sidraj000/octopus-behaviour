The problem is arithmetic, not accuracy

Most of the footage is an empty tank

The 892 hours of colour-camera footage held for one adult Octopus vulgaris is roughly 160,000 twenty-second clips. Labelling all of them with a frontier vision–language model, five passes for reliability, costs 372–1,030 h of wall-clock and an API the aquarium does not have.

And cephalopods spend most of their time hidden or motionless. Prompting an expensive model uniformly spends most of its budget confirming that nothing is happening. The fix is not a better model — it is putting the cheap questions first.

Segmentation · 3.2 M parameters, no prompt

What the small model sees

Skeletonisation

Mask to anatomy

The silhouette is thinned into an anatomical graph — mantle, head and eight arms — then tracked across frames with an optical-flow prior.

Every node is marked detected, fitted or occluded, so kinematics are computed only from evidence-backed samples. Before that distinction, 41.7% of arm samples were evidence-free holds silently entering the motion statistics.

What the pipeline found

The models were never the point

Across 3,083 clips with a visible animal — a behavioural time series no one had for this animal before.

45%

A strongly diurnal animal

Visible activity runs 1–5% overnight and peaks at 45% at 17:00, on a 13:00–19:00 plateau. This only appears once you normalise by how many windows were examined each hour — without that, the curve just traces recording coverage.

2×

Humans nearly double motion

Clips containing a person show mean motion 0.095 against 0.045 at baseline, and arousal 0.68 against 0.46. The clean contrast is human present versus absent — the tank's permanent toys fire an “enrichment” label on two thirds of clips and mean nothing.

41 / 33

Exploration over rest

41% exploration and manipulation, 33% resting, 14% human or enrichment interaction, 9% reaching out of water, 3% locomotion.

The cross-check

Two halves agree without seeing each other

The kinematics pipeline never reads the behaviour label. The teacher never sees a mask. Yet raw arm-tip speed orders the classes exactly as an ethologist would — resting at 53 px/s rising to reaching out of water at 141 px/s.

Kruskal–Wallis H = 33.2, p = 3.5×10-6, and all five resting-versus-other contrasts survive Holm correction.

A purely geometric measurement recovers the distinction a language model was asked about independently — the strongest evidence that the pipeline measures the animal rather than its own conventions.

Negative results

What did not work, kept on the record

The experimental ledger holds every measurement with its provenance — including the conclusions that had to be withdrawn.

  • ✗
    “Teacher-label quality is the segmentation ceiling” — retracted.

    The evidence for it was train leakage. Clean held-out IoU is flat across teacher quality (0.494 → 0.508 → 0.506), and upgrading the teacher bought nothing. What actually moved it was input resolution and a loss that punishes under-segmentation.

  • ✗
    A +0.043 gain from mask-geometry features was leakage.

    It lived entirely on videos the segmenter had trained on, and vanished once holdout videos were excluded from every training source rather than just the final stage.

  • ✗
    A behaviour classifier on pooled static features lost to its own baseline.

    45% accuracy against a 50% majority class. Static frame features cannot classify behaviour; it needs temporal signal, not more labels.

  • ✗
    Head capacity, rebalancing, upsampling, feature augmentation: all ≈ 0.

    The gain came from the frozen representation (+0.087) — specifically how much of the frame the animal occupies.

  • ⇆
    Temporal fusion trades one metric for the other.

    Smoothing costs 0.10 mask IoU but buys +0.17 presence AUC (0.794 → 0.969). Optical flow is worse on both — so plain smoothing, not motion compensation, is the mechanism.

Limitations

What these numbers do not support

  • One animal, one tank. Every measurement is a single adult O. vulgaris. Cross-animal generalisation is untested and is the principal limitation.
  • The human labels measure agreement, not accuracy. All 456 were collected with the model's suggestion visible on screen; every record carries an assisted flag and every one is true. A blind round is specified but was not run.
  • The mask model is colour-only and must not be run on infrared, which is 35% of the corpus.
  • Den occupancy is under-observed by construction. Time hidden in the den is exactly what the presence gate discards, so the denominator is visible time, not elapsed time.
  • Arm speeds are uncalibrated — pixels per second in crop space, with no pixel-to-centimetre mapping.
